Resources and mapping#

This page lists the courses, docs, and roadmaps behind the plan, and which weeks use each one. Daily lessons link to the exact docs page for that day. The courses are optional extra depth: watch the parts that match the week you're on, not the whole course up front.

How links were checked: every link is tested by tools/check-links.mjs, and the results are in tools/link-report.json. Udemy, Coursera, and LinkedIn Learning pages sometimes refuse automated checks, so a "blocked by site" result there doesn't mean the link is broken.

Udemy Business note: Udemy Business includes most popular Udemy courses, but not all of them. If a 🅤 course isn't in your company's catalog, search Udemy Business for its title or instructor.
